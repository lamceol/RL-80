Quick start

  1. Tap the drum buttons above to play. No need to press Start.
  2. Press Rec and choose Start blank, then tap along. Turn on Click for a metronome and a count-in.
  3. Tap the numbered steps to add or remove hits, then press Start to hear your beat. Undo takes back a mistake, and Share sends your beat as a link.

No sound on iPhone? Flip the silent switch off and turn the volume up.
